Draw and close a territory

Territory Drawer

  • Draw: hold the left mouse button (or one finger) and outline an area. On release the outline closes and the area changes hands.
  • Side: choose Україна or Росія in the legend (keys 1 / 2) — that colour gets painted.
  • Move the map: right mouse button, Space, the ✋ button, or two fingers. Wheel or pinch to zoom.
  • Undo / Redo: Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z. Esc cancels an outline. Your work is saved in this browser.
  • Only land of Ukraine and Russia can be recoloured. The percentages show who holds Ukraine’s internationally recognised territory.
